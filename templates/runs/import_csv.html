How to Use
  1. Select a benchmark and download its questions CSV.
  2. Run the questions through your RAG or model.
  3. Fill in the answer column with responses (A/B/C/D for MCQ, or text).
  4. Upload the answered CSV here — the system evaluates and creates a run.
Tip: The CSV needs at least columns question_id and answer.
Expected CSV Format
question_id,answer
mmlu_0,A
mmlu_1,C
mmlu_2,B
mmlu_3,D

question_id: must match exactly the IDs from the downloaded questions CSV.
answer: letter (A/B/C/D/E) for MCQ, or text for open-ended.
